# John Edward: Grief Counselors on Staff

A frequently-cited claim is that John Edward maintains grief counselors on staff to support clients after readings, given the emotional intensity of connecting with deceased loved ones. Below are three references that document this practice, with dates.

---

## References

### 1. Oklahoma State University academic article (FICS journal, article 1543)

URL: https://ojs.library.okstate.edu/osu/index.php/FICS/article/view/1543/1374

An academic analysis of John Edward's professional practices, written in the context of his 1999 book *One Last Time* and his television series *Crossing Over* (launched 2001). The article states:

> "John Edward (1999) employs a full range of media support to promote his psychic and mediumistic practices… He employs grief counselors for the benefit of his audience on his television [show]… John Edward hires a grief counselor for his [show]."

*Date:* Published in the OSU-hosted FICS journal (specific publication date not in snippet; context places it circa 2001).

### 2. Deadline — October 19, 2015

URL: https://deadline.com/2015/10/crossing-over-john-edward-paranormal-series-electus-1201587793/

In announcing a new paranormal series partnership between John Edward and Electus ("One Last Time"), Deadline reported:

> "In each one-hour episode, Edward will randomly read a group of families looking to connect with their lost loved ones. To help these families find healing and closure, **Edward and his grief team** will provide them with the opportunity to see and speak to their loved ones using exclusive technology."

This directly references his dedicated "grief team" supporting clients during and after readings.

*Date: October 19, 2015.*

### 3. The Washington Post — June 30, 2001

URL: https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/2001/06/30/hot-medium-prime-time-spirit-guide-john-edward/5e4d951d-11da-4cb9-996f-f092758c98d9/

Ylan Q. Mui's profile "Hot Medium: Prime-Time Spirit Guide John Edward" discusses Edward's grief-counselor role and his audience of mourners in the context of his Sci-Fi Channel show *Crossing Over*. The article frames Edward's role within a grief-support context, noting that "Edward's fans are a tight-knit group, most of them bonded by grief."

*Date: June 30, 2001.*

---

## Notes

* References #1 and #2 explicitly state that Edward employs/has a grief team supporting clients.
* Reference #3 discusses the grief-counselor context more broadly.
* The strongest, most explicit evidence for "grief counselors on staff" is the academic article (#1) and the 2015 Deadline announcement of "his grief team" (#2).
