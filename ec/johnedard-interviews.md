# John Edward: Interviews on YouTube (Including Larry King)

John Edward (born October 19, 1969) rose to fame in the early 2000s as the host of *Crossing Over with John Edward* and has remained a fixture of talk shows, podcasts, and web series for over two decades. Below are dated summaries of his interview appearances, starting with interviews found on YouTube, followed by his many appearances with Larry King.

---

## YouTube Interviews

### 1. Sarah Grynberg (A Life of Greatness) — "John Edward: Mediumship, Grief & The Afterlife"

URL: https://youtu.be/ezYHQfBif6Y

Edward's third appearance on Australian host Sarah Grynberg's podcast, published to her YouTube channel in **July 2026** (episode released July 27, 2026). The wide-ranging conversation covers the difference between being a psychic and a medium, how he says he receives messages from loved ones who have passed, the double-blind studies he took part in with researchers from Harvard, Yale, and the University of Arizona, and why he believes mediumship should rest on evidence and integrity rather than blind faith. Grief, healing, and how to support someone navigating loss round out the episode, which also features a reading for Grynberg's friend Ange.

### 2. Jay Shetty (On Purpose) — "#1 Medium John Edward: The TRUTH About Life After Death"

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PiV8OWNRd5w

Podcast episode released **January 28, 2026**; YouTube video published **February 11, 2026**. Edward tells Jay Shetty the story of how he went from skeptic to medium — beginning at age 15 with a reading by psychic Lydia Clara that he set out to debunk — and discusses the survival of consciousness, grief as "the other side of love," skepticism versus cynicism, the clairvoyant/clairaudient/clairsentient ways he claims to receive information, and red flags that can indicate a fake psychic. He also describes what changed for him when his uncle died in 1987 and his mother was later diagnosed with cancer.

### 3. DEAD Talks Podcast (David Ferrugio) — Episode #255 "John Edward on Mediumship, Grief, and Connecting With Loved Ones After Death"

URL: https://youtu.be/trZTSfo4x5A

Episode released **March 30, 2026** (clips posted to the DEAD Talks YouTube channel in April 2026). A raw, honest conversation about grief, mediumship, and what it really means to stay connected to those who have passed. Edward explains why he believes most people don't need a reading to feel connected to their loved ones, discusses working with skeptics and law enforcement, how to spot fake mediums, and shares the story of a reading built around repeated "golf" symbolism that resolved with the word "four." He also recounts his unsettling experiences leading up to September 11, 2001 — a feeling he could not explain, a moment inside the World Trade Center, and what happened the night before he was to appear on *Larry King Live*.

### 4. ITV This Morning (UK) — "Psychic Medium John Edward: I Felt 'Dark Evil' Before 9/11"

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zfbEKBrOqDc

Broadcast on ITV's *This Morning* on **May 27, 2026**; posted to YouTube **June 11, 2026**. Interviewed live on the sofa ahead of his UK tour, Edward retells his skeptic-to-medium origin story, discusses his work with former FBI agent Robert Hilland (the subject of Edward's book *Chasing Evil*), and describes the period from June 2001 to September 11 as a "very dark period" — including urging his wife to move a ballroom-dance competition out of the World Trade Center because of the "dark evil" energy he felt there.

### 5. PBS One-on-One (Steve Adubato) — "Psychic Medium John Edward Helps Bring Closure to Loved Ones"

URL: https://www.pbs.org/video/psychic-medium-john-edward-helps-bring-closure-loved-ones-0x/

PBS episode **2128**, aired **April 26, 2018** (available on YouTube and PBS video). Steve Adubato talks with Edward about when he realized he had the ability to connect people with loved ones who have "crossed over" and how that talent carried him to where he is today.

## Larry King Interviews

Edward appeared on *Larry King Live* many times beginning in 1998 (his segment promoting *One Last Time* was so popular the show's switchboard was overwhelmed with calls), and later on *Larry King Now*. The dated appearances below are the most documented.

### 1. Larry King Live — March 6, 2001 (TIME magazine debate)

URL: https://nypost.com/2001/03/08/in-deadlyearnest-time-mag-accuses-up-and-comer-edward-of-faking-it/

The night TIME magazine published Leon Jaroff's critical article "Talking to the Dead" (which called Edward's work "a sophisticated form of the game 20 Questions"), Edward appeared on *Larry King Live* alongside Jaroff, Sylvia Browne, James Van Praagh, skeptic Paul Kurtz, and others. In a heated exchange, Edward denied claims that he uses planted audience members, microphones, and deceptive editing, insisting the show is "edited for time, not for content."

### 2. Larry King Live — September 10, 2001 ("Can John Edward Speak to the Dead?")

URL: https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/lkl/date/2001-09-10/segment/00

The night before the September 11 attacks, Edward was the guest for the full hour, taking phone calls and readings. His appearance has since been discussed in later interviews (see the DEAD Talks episode above) in the context of the unsettling feelings he says he experienced in the months before 9/11. That night he discussed his book *Crossing Over: The Stories Behind the Stories* (No. 3 on the *New York Times* bestseller list that week) and defended his work against cold-reading accusations.

### 3. Larry King Live — September 6, 2002

URL: https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/lkl/date/2002-09-06/segment/00

Edward discussed the third year of *Crossing Over*, follow-ups with people who had received readings, and the *Saturday Night Live* parody of him, which he said he loved — noting, "You've made it when they do it."

### 4. Larry King Live — February 28, 2003

URL: https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/lkl/date/2003-02-28/segment/00

A call-in hour in which Edward gave readings to callers and studio staff, and fielded Larry King's questions — including "What do dead people do all day?" Edward also discussed his Catholic upbringing and how he frames the afterlife in terms of "the survival of consciousness and love."

### 5. Larry King Live — October 2, 2003 ("Interview With John Edward")

URL: https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/lkl/date/2003-10-02/segment/00

Promoting his book *After Life: Answers From the Other Side* (No. 4 on the *New York Times* bestseller list), for which King himself wrote the foreword. King read from the foreword, noting he is "a pure agnostic" but that Edward "often amazes me." Edward took phone calls from around the world, including callers from South Africa.

### 6. Larry King Live — March 7, 2008 ("It's the Psychic Hour")

URL: https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1193873/

A "psychic hour" edition of *Larry King Live* featuring Edward alongside other psychics.

### 7. Larry King Now — August 29, 2012 ("Psychics" with John Edward and Char Margolis)

URL: https://www.dailymotion.com/video/x2rm407

After CNN, King's web series *Larry King Now* (Ora TV) reunited him with Edward and fellow medium Char Margolis. Larry talks with the two about mediums, the afterlife, incorrect readings, and predictions for the future, while they connect with members of the studio audience.
