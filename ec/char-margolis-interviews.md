# Char Margolis: Interviews

Char Margolis is an American psychic medium and author who has parlayed a homespun "everyone is intuitive" pitch into a four-decade media career spanning TV, radio, books, podcasts, and Las Vegas residencies. She claims her abilities surfaced in childhood (she says she saw spirits and "read thoughts" from around age 8), and she got her start in North America on Canadian daytime TV in the late 1980s — The Dini Petty Show, plus Camilla Scott's and Vicki Gabereau's CBC programs — before becoming a fixture of U.S. and European talk shows. Below are dated summaries of her interview appearances, background, and her recurring collaboration with fellow medium John Edward.

## Background

**Signature claim to fame:** In a November 1, 2000 appearance on *Live with Regis*, she told a then-unknown fill-in co-host, Kelly Ripa, that her late grandmother was predicting "a new baby" — days before Ripa learned she was pregnant. Ripa has retold the story for years and hosted her again on *Let's Talk Off Camera* (August 6, 2025).

**Books:** *Questions from Earth, Answers from Heaven* (2000), *Love Karma*, *Discover Your Inner Wisdom*, *The Universe Is Calling You* (2020), and *You Are Psychic* (2022).

**Career arc:** Starred in her own Dutch prime-time show *Char* on RTL 4 from 2002 to 2010; hosted the *CharVision* podcast since 2015; ran the WOW Presents Plus series *Reading Queens with Char Margolis* (2019); appeared on *RuPaul's Drag Race* (March 12, 2021); and headlined a residency at Sahara Las Vegas (September 16, 2023).

## Television and Podcast Interviews (dated)

### 1. Live with Regis and Kelly — "Psychic Week" (November 1, 2000)

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p1adU2ph6Yo

Margolis, reading fill-in co-host Kelly Ripa, picked up the letter "D" (Ripa's grandmother, called "Dee"), then said her late grandmother was "watching over" her and that "there's another baby coming" — which Ripa, secretly two months pregnant, had not told anyone. Ripa recounted the story on *Live with Kelly and Mark* (February 12, 2020) and has hosted Margolis repeatedly since, including on her podcast *Let's Talk Off Camera* (August 6, 2025), where Margolis took listener calls live.

### 2. Larry King Live — multiple appearances (May 5, 2000; July 9, 2004; 2006; July 10, 2008)

URL: https://cnn.com/TRANSCRIPTS/0005/05/lkl.00.html

Margolis was a recurring *Larry King Live* guest alongside fellow mediums (James Van Praagh, Sylvia Browne) and skeptics (Dr. Marvin Farha, Rabbi Shmuley Boteach), doing live phone readings and defending mediumship. Larry King later said of her: "Char shared her gift on my TV show for the first time years ago and she was amazing... and I still don't know how she does it."

### 3. Char (RTL 4, Netherlands) — 2002 to 2010

A prime-time Dutch show built around her readings, running multiple seasons. In 2005, Dutch TV personality Henk Westbroek wrote a column ("Char, Char, Charlatan") claiming she used cold reading and hot reading techniques during his appearance, which he said turned him into an unbeliever.

### 4. An Evening with John Edward and Special Guests — Monmouth University (August 20, 2011)

## Connection to John Edward ("JE")

Margolis and Edward are longtime colleagues who have repeatedly shared stages, talk-show couches, and microphones:

* **August 20, 2011** — "An Evening with John Edward and Special Guests," Monmouth University Pollak Theatre (with Jonathan Louis).
* **August 29, 2012** — *Larry King Now*: "Psychics with John Edward and Char Margolis."
* **November 15, 2012** — Both read studio guests on Katie Couric's *Katie*.
* **October 9, 2015 and April 14, 2017** — Edward was a two-time guest on her *CharVision* podcast.
* **2017** — Edward told *The Edge Magazine* that he built on the work of predecessors including "George Anderson, Suzanne Northrup, Char Margolis."

---

## Criticism and Skeptical Coverage

* **Henk Westbroek, "Char, Char, Charlatan" (DePers.nl, 2005):** The Dutch TV personality claimed she used cold reading and hot reading techniques on him during her RTL 4 show and said the experience turned him into an unbeliever.
* **Maclean's (2010):** Reported she charged US$600 for a 45-minute phone reading and US$825 for an in-person consultation.
* **Skeptical press:** TVmaze and other outlets describe her claims as consistent with standard cold reading/hot reading; her *Larry King Live* phone readings (e.g., July 9, 2004) show the fishing-for-initials pattern critics cite.
