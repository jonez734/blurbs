# Howie Mandel Does Stuff: Psychic Medium Guests

Comedian Howie Mandel's podcast *Howie Mandel Does Stuff* (co-hosted with his daughter Jackelyn Shultz) has repeatedly hosted psychic mediums — a recurring theme the show leans into especially around Halloween. Below are the medium appearances found at this writing, with links to the episodes where available.

---

## Medium Guests

### 1. Amie Balesky — "Jeffrey Epstein Did NOT Kill Himself" (August 31, 2021)

URL: https://pocketcasts.com/podcast/howie-mandel-does-stuff-podcast/9f1d7130-5d42-0139-33dc-0acc26574db2/jeffrey-epstein-did-not-kill-himself/7111a349-6cc4-498a-8f36-67178dfc54ea

Psychic medium Amie Balesky joins Howie and Jackie for a "spiritual journey" in which the trio communicate with a deceased figure — the episode is billed around internet tea and talking to the dead, with several emotional moments. *Date: August 31, 2021.*

### 2. George Anderson — "Howie Talks to Ghosts" (#94, November 10, 2022)

URL: no stable link located at this writing

George Anderson, billed as "the world's most scientifically tested medium," contacts a relative of Howie's who recently passed. The episode was posted November 10, 2022. *Date: November 10, 2022.*

### 3. John Edward — "John Edward Shocked Us With Real Ghost Interaction Caught on Camera" (#145, October 31, 2023)

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b6CiPPtuYEI

Edward's first appearance on the show, released as a Halloween special. Edward recounts his origin story and how he says his gift works, then performs an in-studio reading — with what the show frames as a real ghost interaction caught on camera. *Date: October 31, 2023.*

*See also: [John Edward: Interviews](./john-edward-interviews.md)*

### 4. John Edward & Robert Hilland of the FBI — "Psychic Medium John Edward & Robert Hilland of the FBI" (September 2, 2025)

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24Ej8IchiW4

Edward returned to the show alongside former FBI Special Agent Robert Hilland to discuss *Chasing Evil*, the book documenting how the skeptical agent reached out to Edward for help on a baffling case and the twenty-five-year crime-solving partnership that followed. *Date: September 2, 2025.*

*See also: [John Edward: Interviews](./john-edward-interviews.md)*

### 5. Two psychics with Audrina Patridge & Michael Ray — "Audrina Patridge & Boyfriend Michael Ray Join Two Psychics for Huge Release & Announcement" (June 10, 2025)

URL: https://www.iheart.com/podcast/269-howie-mandel-does-stuff-po-78028868/episode/audrina-patridge-boyfriend-michael-ray-280196318/

Reality TV star Audrina Patridge and country singer Michael Ray join the show alongside two psychics for what the episode bills as a "huge release & announcement." *Date: June 10, 2025.*

### 6. Matt Fraser — "Matt Fraser Medium, The Most Unbelievable Episode Yet" (#237, May 13, 2025)

URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fpn7Vy97siE

Psychic medium Matt Fraser, star of *Meet the Frasers* and author of *We Never Die*, joins the show for live readings that Howie bills as "the most unbelievable episode yet." Fraser has described Howie Mandel as a friend and believer who has become "a great friend" through his readings. *Date: May 13, 2025.*
