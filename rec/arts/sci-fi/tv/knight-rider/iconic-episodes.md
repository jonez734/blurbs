# Iconic Episodes: Knight Rider

Knight Rider ran for four seasons (1982-1986) and produced 90 episodes. These three stand out as particularly memorable.

## Series Finale

Knight Rider **did not have a proper series finale**. The show was cancelled after Season 4 due to rising production costs, and NBC aired the intended finale out of order.

The intended finale was **"The Scent of Roses"** (Season 4, Episode 12, January 3, 1986). David Hasselhoff, knowing the show was ending, helped conceive this story with his then-wife Catherine Hickland (who played Stephanie Mason). In this episode, Michael nearly dies, decides to quit the Foundation, reunites with Stephanie, and they marry. But then the villain returns and Stephanie takes a bullet for Michael—dying in his arms. He avenges her, then drives off with KITT to "go home, to family, and to the Foundation." It was a proper emotional conclusion.

However, NBC instead aired **"Voo Doo Knight"** (Season 4, Episode 22, April 4, 1986) as the final episode—a silly plot about a voodoo princess hypnotizing men, with no sense of closure or farewell to the characters.

Like The A-Team, Knight Rider simply... ended, leaving fans without a proper goodbye to Michael and KITT.

- **[Knight of the Juggernaut](iconic-episodes/knight-of-the-juggernaut)** — S4E1 (Sep 20, 1985). RC3 debuts, KITT destroyed by Juggernaut vehicle, rebuilt with new features.

- **[Junk Yard Dog](iconic-episodes/junk-yard-dog)** — S3E14 (Feb 3, 1985). KITT destroyed in acid pit, rebuilt by original design team.

- **[Knight Song](iconic-episodes/knight-song)** — S4E10 & 11 (Dec 13, 1985). New Edition guest stars, blind piano player, neighborhood harassment, Charley Conners drives KITT.
