# Michael Carbonaro

Born April 28, 1976, in Oakdale, New York, Michael Carbonaro is best known as the star of truTV's "The Carbonaro Effect" (premiered May 15, 2014), a hidden-camera magic show where he pranks unsuspecting strangers with mind-bending illusions.

## Early Life & Education

Michael is the younger of two sons of an electrician father and nurse mother. He attended Connetquot High School in Bohemia, New York, and later earned a bachelor's degree in drama from the Tisch School of the Arts at New York University.

## Before Magic Career

Originally, Michael wanted to be a special effects and makeup artist. He bought supplies at a magic shop, discovered magic there, and became hooked at age 8-9. His first professional show came at age 13, performing at birthday parties as a clown. He performed magic to pay for his college tuition at NYU.

## Television Career

Before "The Carbonaro Effect," Michael appeared in numerous television shows and films, including "Chappelle's Show," "Another Gay Movie," "All My Children," "Guiding Light," "Law & Order: SVU," "30 Rock," "CSI: Miami," "Grey's Anatomy," and "The Newsroom."

From 2003 to 2014, he had a recurring segment called "Magic Clerk" on "The Tonight Show with Jay Leno," where he would perform magic for staff and crew members at the NBC studios.
